# Privacy Policy — MK Patch Librarian

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

MK Patch Librarian does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and makes no network connections of any kind.

Everything the app works with stays on your Mac:

- **Patch data** you load from your synthesizer or import from files is stored locally in the app's own sandboxed container so your last bank is still there the next time you open the app.
- **Preferences**, such as a preferred MIDI port, are stored locally using standard macOS preferences.
- **Files you import** are read only when you choose them in the Open dialog or drag them into the window. The app cannot access any other files.
- **MIDI communication** happens only between your Mac and the MIDI devices connected to it.

Deleting the app removes its container and everything stored in it.

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/otonomee/mk-patch-librarian/issues.
